Who we are

Your favourite spot for refreshment.

At Girish Cold Drinks you’ll find chilled lassi, milk shakes, fresh fruit juices, faluda, thandai and cold coco — along with Havmore ice cream scoops, shrikhand, refreshing drinks and desserts.

It’s a family business that started with a lemon-water cart in Ahmedabad’s Manek Chowk and carries the name of the founder’s son, Girish.
