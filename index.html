Our Story

From a lemon-water cart to C. G. Road.

Girish Cold Drinks began with a simple lemon-water cart in Ahmedabad’s Manek Chowk, set up by Vashrambhai Thakkar to support his family. The name comes from his son, Girish.

  1. A cart in Manek ChowkLemon water, sold from a handcart outside a shop.
  2. A name in the familyThe shop was named after Girish, the founder’s son.
  3. Run by the family todayGenerations later, the family still runs the business.
