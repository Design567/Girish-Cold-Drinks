Our Story

From a lemon-water cart in Manek Chowk to Girish Cold Drinks on C. G. Road — a family business, generation after generation.

How it began

Four chapters of Girish.

  1. A lemon-water cart in Manek Chowk

    To support his family, Vashrambhai Thakkar began selling lemon water from a handcart in Manek Chowk, Ahmedabad.

  2. A shop to call their own

    The family had come to Ahmedabad from Saurashtra. The owner of the shop beside the cart gave it to Vashrambhai, even though he had no money to buy it.

  3. Named after Girish

    Vashrambhai had six sons. The shop took the name of his second son — Girish.

  4. A family business today

    The family’s third generation now runs the business. On C. G. Road, the crossroads by our shop is known locally as “Girish Cold Drinks char rasta”.
